What the app stores

Everything you enter — investments, cash, assets, loans, exchange rates and settings — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to any server we run.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share any personal or financial information.

Backups and PDFs

If you use "Save backup" or "Save PDF", the file is saved to your device. Where you keep or send that file afterwards is up to you.

Internet use

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts when online. No data you enter is included in these requests.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete all investments" in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all data permanently, so save a backup first if you want to keep it.

Not financial advice

Tax figures are estimates for information only. Check with a chartered accountant before acting.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
